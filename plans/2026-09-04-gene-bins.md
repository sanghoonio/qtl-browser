# Gene bins and gene_detail

From `2026-09-04-r2-parquet-audit.md`: after the range-read fix a cold gene page is ~44
requests, dominated by the per-chromosome nominal footer (874 KB on chr7, 1.9 MB on chr1,
read in 3–5 growing ranges) and by four separate small tables (genes, exons, splice
phenotypes, neighbours) each costing a footer plus 2–3 row groups.

## Change

1. **Bins.** Every tested gene gets `bin` = its rank by TSS within its chromosome, integer
   divided by `nominal_bin_genes` (100). Stored in `genes.parquet` and `search_index` (which
   the app holds in memory), so the app never computes a bin from coordinates. Untested genes
   have no bin.
2. **Nominal tables** are written to `cis_{e,s}qtl_nominal/chr=<chr>/bin=<bin>/data.parquet`,
   still one row group per gene, same encodings. About 200 eQTL files and up to 200 sQTL
   files (only bins holding a gene with a significant intron) instead of 23 each. Footers
   drop from 0.9–1.9 MB to roughly 40–90 KB.
3. **`gene_detail`**: one row per tested gene with the full `genes` row plus two list
   columns, `exons` (collapsed model, `list<struct<start,end>>`) and `splice` (every tested
   intron with its permutation result). Partitioned `chr=/bin=`, one row group per gene,
   statistics on `gene_id`. A gene page reads one footer and one row group for everything
   except the locus.
4. **`search_index`** gains `start`, `end`, `strand`, `biotype`, so the neighbour genes for
   the gene track come from memory.
5. **App**: `geneDetail(hit)` replaces `geneRow` + `splicePhenotypes` + `collapsedExons` +
   `genesInWindow` on the gene page; nominal paths come from `hit.bin`; the variant page's
   cis scan reads the bins of genes whose TSS is within 1 Mb; `db.ts` registers the bin files
   from the search index after it loads. `genes.parquet`, `exons.parquet`,
   `splice_phenotypes.parquet` stay for the variant and region pages.
6. Old per-chromosome nominal files move to `data/derived/_full/nominal_by_chr/` locally.
   Nothing is uploaded until the local check passes; the old keys in the bucket stay until a
   `sync --delete`.

## Decisions & ownership

| # | Decision | Owner / status | Rationale and what it forces |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Do D and G | user-owned, confirmed 2026-09-04 | Sam picked these from the audit; test locally before any bucket write. |
| D2 | Bins by gene rank (100 genes) rather than fixed base pairs | AI-owned, defended | Equal-sized footers regardless of gene density; needs the bin stored in the search index, which the app already loads. Forces: the bin is a pipeline artifact, and a gene's file changes if the tested-gene set changes. |
| D3 | `gene_detail` holds exons and splice phenotypes, not credible sets | AI-owned, defended | Credible sets now come from the locus window (audit H). Splice phenotypes are needed before the locus. |
| D4 | Keep `genes`, `exons`, `splice_phenotypes` as separate tables | AI-owned, defended | Variant and region pages look up by position, not by gene; duplicating ~15 MB in the bucket is cheaper than re-plumbing them. |
| D5 | Bin file list derived from `search_index` at boot, not hard-coded | AI-owned, defended | ~600 names; the index already knows every (chr, bin). Forces: registration happens after the index loads, before the first gene query. |
| D6 | Untested genes have no `gene_detail` row and no bin | AI-owned, default | Their page shows only the "not tested" notice. |

## What this changes elsewhere

- **Layout rule in `pipeline/README.md`** ("hive-partitioned by chromosome") becomes
  chromosome + bin for the nominal tables and `gene_detail`.
- **Bucket**: ~400 new small files plus `gene_detail` (~20 MB); the 46 old nominal keys
  (5.3 GB) are dead weight until deleted. Storage briefly ~12 GB if both exist; over the
  10 GB free tier until `sync --delete`.
- **`validate`**: row-count check globs bins per chromosome; the FLNC pruning check reads
  FLNC's bin file.
- **Rebuild cost**: `permutation_tables` (genes, search_index) and `nominal` run again
  (~1 min each locally); `gene_detail` is new.
